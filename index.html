What You Get — And What It Is NOT

Engineered specifically to solve the first 2 minutes without complication.

What the First-Reply System Does

  • Auto-reply within 2 minutes

    Guaranteed response day or night, weekdays and weekends.

  • Personalised reply

    Uses the enquirer's name, your verified business name, and your specific offer.

  • Shows your available slots

    Presents your real timeslots so prospective clients can take action immediately.

  • Notifies you instantly

    Sends an alert to your personal WhatsApp with full lead details and conversation context.

  • Seamless handoff

    Bot steps aside the moment you reply. You stay completely in control of your clients.

  • Opt-out compliant

    Automatically handles STOP and UNSUBSCRIBE requests immediately.

  • Leads log & audit trail

    Inspect every inbound enquiry, timestamp, response time, and SLA record.

  • No invented facts

    System strictly uses information you approve. Never invents prices, hours, or promises.

What It Is NOT

  • Not a replacement for you

    It does not pretend to be you or run your company — it buys you 2 minutes to respond properly.

  • Not a complex chatbot

    No fragile conversational loops or confusing AI menus. It does one thing exceptionally well: the first reply.

  • Not locked to a platform

    You own your configuration, logs, and data at all times. No proprietary lock-in.
