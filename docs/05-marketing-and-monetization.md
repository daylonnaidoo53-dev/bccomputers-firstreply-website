## 1. Value Proposition & Positioning

### The Pitch
Most AI reply apps charge $20 to $50/month, sell your prompts to cloud providers, or bill per token. **FirstReply PC flips that model:**
- **Zero-dollar core forever**: Runs locally on open models (Ollama) or free cloud developer allowances.
- **Privacy native**: Runs on your desktop. Your business leads and emails never touch our servers because we don't have servers.
- **2-second turnaround**: Highlight any message, press `Ctrl+Shift+R`, pick your tone, review the draft, and paste.

### Target Audience
1. **Local Business Owners & Solo Operators**: Gyms, wellness studios, trades, accountants, and consultants handling dozens of WhatsApp and email inquiries daily.
2. **Privacy-Conscious Professionals**: Lawyers, doctors, and financial advisors who cannot paste client messages into public SaaS AI tools.
3. **Productivity Power Users**: Anyone sick of writing "Thanks for reaching out, when are you free for a call?" fifty times a week.
